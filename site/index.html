How these numbers are made

  • Jobs come from public company career pages (Greenhouse and Ashby boards) that Unlisted reads every hour. This is not every job on the market. Large companies that use other systems are missing.
  • A skill counts when its name appears in the job's title or description, using a list of 85 skills. A mention is not always a requirement.
  • The type of role comes from the job title. Titles that match no type are counted as "Other".
  • Weeks start on Monday and count jobs by the day they were posted. The newest week is still filling up, so it is drawn as a dashed line.
